# Privacy Policy for Neribo Vault

**Last updated:** 3 October 2026

Neribo Vault ("the app") is a private notebook app for Android made by Westly Ibiese, Benin City, Nigeria ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

- Your notes, ideas, goals, diary, stories, posts, church records, memories, documents and developer items are stored **on your phone**.
- The app works fully offline. It does not show ads and has no analytics or tracking.
- **Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to sign in and turn on cloud sync.**
- Developer secrets never leave your phone, even with cloud sync on.

## 1. Information stored on your device

Everything you write or add is kept in the app's private storage on your phone:

- Text you create in the vaults: Notes, Ideas, Goals, Diary, Writers (stories, chapters, characters), Posts, Church records, Memories, Documents, and the Developer vault (projects, bugs, tasks, plans, prompts, documents, secrets).
- Photos you add to Memories and files or photos you attach to Documents. These are copied into the app's private storage.
- Your settings, such as theme and which vaults are locked.
- Your app PIN and vault PINs. These are stored only as protected hashes, not as the PIN itself. If you turn on fingerprint or face unlock, the unlocking is done by Android. The app never receives your fingerprint or face data.
- Developer secrets are encrypted on the device with a key derived from your six-digit Secrets PIN.
- A local activity log of actions inside the Developer vault. It stays on your phone.

Deleted items go to "Recently deleted" and are removed permanently after 30 days.

## 2. Optional cloud sync

Cloud sync is **off until you sign in**. If you choose to sign in:

- You sign in with your **Google account**. Google shows its own sign-in sheet. The app receives a sign-in token from Google, which it exchanges for a session with the Neribo cloud. The app stores your Google email address and a user ID for your session.
- The text of the vaults you leave switched on is copied to the **Neribo cloud**, a database hosted by Supabase and managed by me. You can turn each vault on or off separately. The **Diary is off by default**.
- **Never synced:** developer secrets, the activity log, and the photos and files you attach (only the text of those items syncs).
- Synced data is **not end-to-end encrypted**. It is protected in transit with HTTPS, and stored by the hosting provider. I do not read your content, and I will not sell it or share it with anyone for advertising.
- The app syncs when you open it and about every six hours while you are online and signed in.
- Signing out stops syncing and keeps all your data on your phone.

The app also contains a setting to store sync data in a Supabase project that you create yourself. If you use that, your data goes to your project and is governed by your Supabase account, not by me.

## 3. Service providers

When you use cloud sync, these providers process data for the app to work:

- **Google**, for sign-in. See Google's privacy policy.
- **Supabase**, for hosting the cloud database. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Beyond this, I do not share your information with anyone. The app has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## 4. Backups you make

In Settings you can create a backup file (.nvbackup) protected by a password you choose. You pick where it is saved, for example Google Drive or your computer. The password cannot be recovered. Where you store the file is up to you and subject to that service's terms.

## 5. Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** only used for optional cloud sync.
- **Notifications:** for reminders you set (posts, documents, tasks). You can refuse this permission and the app still works.
- **Photos and files:** the app uses Android's Photo Picker and file picker, so you choose exactly which photos and files to add. It does not get access to your whole gallery or storage.
- **Biometrics:** only to unlock the app and locked vaults, if you turn it on.

## 6. Screenshots

When a developer secret is on screen, or when you turn on the "block screenshots" option or lock the app, the app asks Android to block screenshots and hide the app's preview in the recent apps list.

## 7. Children

Neribo Vault is a general-purpose app and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used cloud sync, contact me and I will delete the data.

## 8. Keeping and deleting your data

- **On your phone:** delete items in the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove everything.
- **In the Neribo cloud:** signing out does not delete your cloud copy. To have your cloud data and account record deleted, email me at the address above from the Google account you used. I will delete it within a reasonable time. A way to delete your cloud copy from inside the app is not available yet.

## 9. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage, sensitive settings are encrypted on the device, and cloud traffic uses HTTPS. No method is perfectly secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top and, for important changes, say so in the app or the store listing.
