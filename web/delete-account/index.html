Delete your Neribo Vault account and data

Neribo Vault is a private vault app for Android by Westly Ibiese. This page explains how to delete the cloud copy of your data and your account.

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Neribo Vault and go to Settings.
  2. Open Cloud sync.
  3. Scroll to Delete my cloud data and tap Delete my cloud data and account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Deletion from the app happens straight away.

Option 2: ask by email

If you can no longer open the app, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject Delete my Neribo Vault account. I will delete your cloud data and account and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Everything your account synced to the Neribo cloud: the text of your notes, ideas, goals, diary, stories, posts, church records, memories, documents and developer items.
  • Your account record, including the email address and user ID stored for sign-in.

What is not deleted

  • Anything stored on your own phone. Your vaults, photos, files and developer secrets never leave the phone, so delete them inside the app, or uninstall it.
  • Your Google account. Neribo Vault only uses it to sign you in.
  • Backup files (.nvbackup) you saved yourself, wherever you keep them.
